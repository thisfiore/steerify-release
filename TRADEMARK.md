# Steerify name and logo

The Steerify source code is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE). You can use it, change it,
fork it and ship your own version.

The **Steerify name, logo and app icon** are not part of that license. They belong to Francesco Fiore
and identify the official project and its releases.

## Fine without asking

- Saying your project works with, is built on, or is forked from Steerify.
- Using the name in articles, talks, tutorials, videos and reviews.
- Linking to the project and showing unmodified screenshots of the app.

## Please don't

- Distribute a modified version under the name Steerify, or under a name close enough to be confused
  with it. Forks need their own name and icon.
- Use the name or logo for a paid product, hosted service or company without written permission.
- Suggest that your project is official, endorsed or sponsored by Steerify when it is not.

## Questions

Not sure whether a use is fine? Open an issue at https://github.com/thisfiore/steerify/issues and ask.
