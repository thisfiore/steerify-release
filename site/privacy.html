Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: your boards and code stay with you. The site counts visits without cookies. We keep your email only if you give it to us.

Last updated 23 November 2026

Who we are

Steerify is an independent project made by Frenk. "We" on this page means Frenk. We decide how the data below is used and are responsible for it.

The website (steerify.dev)

  • Visit counts. Each page sends one anonymous signal: the page, the page you came from, your screen width, your time zone, your country, and clicks on downloads and outside links. No cookies. We don't keep your IP address. To count unique visitors, we combine your IP address and browser into a one-way code that changes every day.
  • Sign-up forms. If you ask for updates, the Windows waitlist or an iPhone TestFlight invite, we keep your email, which form you used, and the page you were on. If our own server is down, the form goes to Formspree, which stores it for us.
  • Third parties the pages load. Fonts come from Google Fonts. The latest version number comes from GitHub. Both see your IP address, as any website you open does.

The Mac app

  • Your boards, projects and Claude Code sessions stay on your Mac and in your own git repositories. We never receive them.
  • To check for updates, the app reads a release list on GitHub. GitHub sees your IP address and app version.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting.

The iPhone app

  • It reads your board straight from your GitHub repository. It uses the GitHub token you pair it with, and that token stays in the iPhone's Keychain.
  • To keep your Mac and iPhone in sync, they send short signals ("go read GitHub now", which sessions are running) through our relay at relay.steerify.dev. The relay keeps them in memory only and never stores your board.
  • Beta testing goes through Apple TestFlight. When we invite you, your email goes to Apple. Apple's privacy policy covers TestFlight.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Why we use it

  • Email: to send you what you asked for (updates, the waitlist, a TestFlight invite). This rests on your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Visit counts: to see which pages help. This rests on our legitimate interest, and no one can be identified from them.

Where it is kept, and for how long

  • On DigitalOcean servers in Frankfurt (EU), and at Formspree for fallback sign-ups.
  • Your email: until you ask us to remove it, or until we stop using the list.
  • Visit counts: as totals per day, without who visited.
  • We don't sell or share your data, and we don't use it for ads.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete what we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Ask us and it's done, usually within a few days. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Steerify is a tool for developers and is not meant for children under 16.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/thisfiore/steerify-release, or reply to any email we sent you. If this page changes, the date above changes too.
