How Steerify works

From one sentence to shipped.

The whole idea in 45 seconds. No screenshots, no manual.

  1. Say what you want. Write it in plain words. Steerify turns it into requirements, docs and a roadmap.
  2. Confirm it with pictures. Before anything is built, Claude shows the options as mockups. You pick the one you meant.
  3. Build the right thing. Each answer starts a Claude session, already briefed, in the right repo.
  4. Always in sync. Every session sits on its roadmap item. The status moves on its own.
  5. Only what needs you. Nine sessions running, three waiting on you. You see those three.
  6. One standard. Every delivery is checked against the same design and the same tests.
  7. Skills, not repeats. What works becomes a skill. Every session reuses it, so everything stays aligned.
  8. You just steer. You choose, look and say ship. Claude does the rest.
