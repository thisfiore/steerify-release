    Read the seven chapters

    1. Hold the wheel

      Steerify keeps your Claude Code sessions next to a board: what to decide, what to look at, what to ship. Claude builds. You steer. Someone has to.

    2. Sessions in sync with the roadmap

      Every roadmap item has a Start button. The session opens in the right repo, already briefed, and the item shows its status as the work moves. No orphan terminals.

    3. Choices, with pictures

      When Claude needs a decision, it asks on the board, with a mockup for every option and one marked as suggested. Pick one, or write a note. That's the meeting.

    4. Visual review

      Finished work lands in To test as pictures. Press ⌘ and click to pin a note exactly where it's wrong. Refine goes back to the session that built it.

    5. Organised progress

      Every item moves from choose, to build, to test, to shipped. The progress bar fills itself. Nobody updates it by hand, least of all you.

    6. Say OK. It ships.

      What you approved waits in Release. One click, and the session commits its own changes, deploys, and marks it live.

    7. Never get lost

      Lost? Press ⌘J. Steerify shows the one thing waiting on you, in any project. Read less. Ship more.
